Welcome

A quiet corner of Limpopo, built for rest and for gathering.

The Elephant Lodge is a peaceful accommodation, function venue and conference facility set in Sedimothole, Seleteng, Ga‑Mphahlele — with the mountains as a backdrop and thatch, stone and warm light throughout. Whether you're staying the night, planning a celebration, or hosting a meeting, the lodge is arranged to make each of those things easy.

Guests can book a room for the night, take up one of our monthly rental rooms for singles or couples, hire the thatched function venue for a private gathering, or reserve the conference room for corporate workshops — with secure, walled and electric-fenced grounds throughout.
