Our Story

About The Elephant Lodge

A family-run retreat built around the rhythm of the Limpopo bushveld.

Who We Are

Rooted in Ga‑Mphahlele

The Elephant Lodge sits in Sedimothole, Seleteng, Ga‑Mphahlele, Limpopo — a walled, gated property built to give guests a genuine sense of quiet, with the mountains rising just beyond the boundary wall. What began as an accommodation stop has grown into a small hospitality complex offering overnight rooms, monthly rentals, a function venue and a conference facility.

Our name and mark take their shape from the African continent itself — a reminder that, however small the lodge, it sits within something much bigger: the land, the community, and the travellers who pass through it.

Our Approach

Practical hospitality, done properly

We keep our offering focused: comfortable rooms, a well-equipped function hall, and a conference room that actually works for a day of meetings. Every space is serviced, Wi‑Fi connected, and set within secure, electric-fenced grounds — because peace of mind is as important as thatch and mountain views.

Whether you're here for one night, one month, or one event, our team aims to make the booking simple and the stay comfortable.

The Grounds

A secure, self-contained property

The lodge complex includes guest rooms, a thatched function hall, a conference room, a swimming pool and braai area, and landscaped gardens — all inside walled, electric-fenced grounds with controlled access.

  • Walled property with electric fencing
  • Landscaped gardens and paved courtyards
  • Swimming pool with braai and seating area
  • On-site parking
  • Set against the backdrop of the Limpopo mountains
