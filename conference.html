The Room

A focused space, away from the city

The conference facility offers a quiet, dedicated space equipped with a whiteboard and flip chart — suited to strategy sessions, training workshops, and small to mid-size corporate meetings.

Paired with our function hall for larger plenary sessions and our accommodation for overnight or multi-day workshops, The Elephant Lodge can host your event from arrival to departure.

  • Whiteboard and flip chart provided
  • Flexible table layouts (boardroom or classroom style)
  • Natural light with a calm, private setting
  • Combine with the function hall for larger sessions
  • On-site accommodation for multi-day events
