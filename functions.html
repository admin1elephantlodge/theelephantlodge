The Venue

Space that adapts to your occasion

Our function venue is a vaulted, thatched-roof hall that can be arranged in a boardroom, banquet, or open-floor layout depending on your event. It comfortably hosts private parties, birthdays, memorials, engagements and community functions.

Tables can be dressed in your choice of linen, and the hall opens out onto the lodge's gardens and paved courtyard — a natural spot for photos or an outdoor reception area.

  • Vaulted thatch-roof hall with ambient lighting
  • Flexible seating layouts for private or community events
  • Linen-covered tables and chairs available
  • Adjoining garden and courtyard for outdoor moments
  • On-site parking within secure, walled grounds
